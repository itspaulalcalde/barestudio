Materials & Inks

A selection of high-quality, skin-friendly and thoughtfully sourced materials.

We use high-quality, vegan and cruelty-free inks, carefully selected for their safety and performance.

Our inks are chosen to create precise, long-lasting results while respecting your skin.

We use high-quality, sterile and single-use needles, carefully selected to ensure precision, comfort and safety throughout every tattoo session.

Our fine needles allow us to create delicate lines and detailed designs.

Every design begins with a precise and carefully prepared stencil.

We use skin-friendly transfer papers that ensure clean, detailed outlines and accurate placement, helping us achieve the best possible result.

Taking care of your tattoo is an essential part of the process.

We provide personalized aftercare guidance and recommend gentle products to help your tattoo heal properly.
